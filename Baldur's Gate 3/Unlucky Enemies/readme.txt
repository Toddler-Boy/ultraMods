Unlucky Enemies for Baldur's Gate 3

What it does
Enemies roll a natural 1 against your party and the allies fighting at your side:
- every enemy attack on them is a critical miss, weapon, unarmed and spell attacks alike
- every saving throw an enemy makes against their spells is a natural 1

How it works
A hidden status on every party member holds two automatic reactions that set the enemy's roll to 1 right after it
is rolled, the way the game's own Illusory Self and Portent do. Allies outside the party carry the status while they
are in combat. It works for characters who join the party later, and in saves made before the mod was installed.

Requirements
Norbyte's Baldur's Gate 3 Script Extender.

Install
Install the .pak with the game's mod manager or BG3 Mod Manager and enable it.

Uninstall
Disable or remove it in the mod manager.

Notes
- Enemy ability checks, such as resisting a shove, roll normally.
- Goes well with Always Roll 20, which makes every roll of your party a natural 20.
