Always Roll 20 for Baldur's Gate 3

What it does
Every d20 your party rolls is a natural 20, and so is every d20 of allies fighting at your side:
- attack rolls are critical hits, unarmed attacks and spell attacks included
- ability checks and skill checks pass, in dialogue too
- saving throws pass: a natural 20 alone does not pass a save in combat, so saves also get +10
- damage dice roll their maximum
- initiative gets +20, so the party acts first in every fight
- death saving throws get +20

How it works
A hidden status on every party member holds the game's own roll boosts (the ones Reliable Talent and Destructive
Wrath use), and an automatic reaction sets each attack roll to 20 right after it is rolled, the way the game's own
Portent and Luck of the Far Realms do. Allies outside the party carry the status while they are in combat. It works
for characters who join the party later, and in saves made before the mod was installed.

Requirements
Norbyte's Baldur's Gate 3 Script Extender.

Install
Install the .pak with the game's mod manager or BG3 Mod Manager and enable it.

Uninstall
Disable or remove it in the mod manager.

Notes
- Goes well with Unlucky Enemies, which makes every enemy roll a 1.
